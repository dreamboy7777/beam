Like Discord, but yours.

This mode sends the whole desktop and its audio through your own Beam relay. No accounts, no TURN, no third-party video service.

  • Send the link first, have your friend open it, then share — lowest latency.
  • Entire screen + Share system audio = everything, with sound.
  • Window sharing usually has no audio.
  • The friend must click Watch with sound once.
  • Keep this machine awake and this tab open.
  • Latency is typically 1–2 seconds; it is a relay, not a gaming stream.
