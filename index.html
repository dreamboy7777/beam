Start a room, send one link, and your friend is watching your screen in seconds. No installs. No accounts. No recordings — the video goes straight between the two of you.

  • True screen shareWhole screen, a window, or one tab — with tab audio.
  • Both directionsYour friend can share their screen back at any time.
  • Encrypted in transitWebRTC media is mandated to use DTLS-SRTP. Nothing is stored.
